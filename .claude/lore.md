## World
- **Rouge Land (เกาะเนื้อ)** — island made of living flesh/bone/organs (a body whose heart still beats). Vintage 1600s–1950s aesthetic, Greek-Roman ruins on beaches. Surrounded by pitch-black blood sea with **Abysmarines**.
- **Cypress Isle** — only gateway to Rouge Land. Filters lost souls. Field of black doors facing south. A cloaked **ferryman** takes you to Rouge Land for a "priceless sacrifice"; you only see him once you've lost enough humanity.
- **Old Realm / Outer Cyclical Realm** — subspace overlapping the real world, stretched 8–10× horizontally (heights unchanged). Real people = frozen translucent "traces". Dungeons hide behind ordinary-looking doors. Body mass slowly drains here ("I have flesh, therefore I am").
- **Fresh flesh** = currency, power, authority. Untainted flesh (newborns, sane newcomers). Only Thanatos's influence rots it.
- **Rotten flesh → Miasma**: hallucinations, mind degradation, mutation. Purple-ish, acid/vinegar stench.
- **Crimson Moon**: blood-red full moon erodes flesh remnants' sanity → Fiends. Everyone shelters indoors.

## M.A.I.D.S (Meat Allocation Identification Distribution Sanctuary)
- Origin: The Great Hunger → mysterious woman with jar of Everlasting Flesh → The Silent Pact (orders from entity in deepest sea). Sacred duty, not business: stop gluttony AND starvation (both → flesh remnant).
- Maid skills: flawless butchery, instant grading/curse detection, vow of abstinence (may eat off duty).
- **Father Damiano** (Eternal Priest of Flesh): Confessional of Culinary Craving, diluted-Miasma conditioning, anatomy drills with kris, Dance of the Butchering Blade (plagal cadence), First Verses suppress icon repercussions.
- **Le Fontaine de Sang**: HQ, French château, turquoise roof/dome chapel, crimson gardens, blood fountain. All branch butcheries' back doors connect to its halls. Dorms named per Icon.
- Grades: **A** Prime Executor · **B** Choice Senior Staff · **C** Select Field Operator (main hunters) · **D** Utility Sanitation (shop clerk, grading, eats substandard flesh, erases evidence/witnesses).
- Secret Record (Sunday gestation ritual) — exists in doc; NOT used in bot content.

## Roles
1. **Maid / Butcher** — cold, elegant, gender-neutral uniforms.
2. **Hound** — flesh hunters for maids, traded humanity for power, mutations, bone weapons. Maid↔Hound friction; maid caring for hound = taboo.
3. **Bar Staff / Bar Owner** — Bar = only sanctuary zone where humans + flesh remnants share a table. Owner in "Unstable State", cooks rotten flesh to mitigate curses, **Closing Shift** (daily involuntary blackout).
4. **Patron / Flesh Remnant** — intelligent ex-humans, wealthy clients, crave fresh flesh, collect real-world vintage items.
5. **Outsider (Lost Soul)** — from modern world via rifts/Miasma/rituals. Carry phone/lighter/photo. Dilemma: eat flesh (lose humanity) or starve.
6. **Rouge Land Survivor** — escaped ex-remnant, mutated, guides rookie hunters.

## Doors (Solomon007's Archive 1)
- Green: womb/hive walls, regenerating infant monsters, endless food.
- Yellow: pitch dark, door locks behind you, need light (lighter!), flee terror.
- Gray: mirrors real world (outlets work, instant food, desire illusions), lose track of time; last door = first door.
- Orange: maws/abysses/tendrils; must feed it flesh to leave.
- White: silent, sealed 3–9 days, no food/water; endurance → shrine.
- Blue: tragic memories; nobody returns whole (body merges with walls).
- Purple: Thanatos Miasma — avoid.
- Grayish-black inside = shortcut. Final chamber: **red** (→ Cypress Isle) or **black** (exit near entrance; dungeon vanishes). You can only see your own Icon's colored door → its shrine.

## Fiends
Ex-remnants with humanity erased; irreversible. Causes: overwhelming icon power / gluttony, Crimson Moon, dungeon-born. Look shaped by former Icon (Sophia=gestation, Belphegor=spikes/stakes, Beelzebub=multi-maws, Hastur=void eyes/long limbs, Loki=geometric shifting, Thanatos=desiccated spore husks).
